The brand library / Review 02

Positioning — working recommendation

17 October 2026 · Working identity

Category: biryani. Master brand: Dum E Odisha. Emotional aim: the warm anticipation of opening a meal. The recommended expression is the lifted lid: a readable name, a small moment of appetite, and a generous food illustration. This is a design frame, not evidence of a cooking method or regional recipe.

Priority use hypothesis: a person meets the name on Instagram, remembers it, then sees consistent identity on a label or carrier. Clear naming and practical dish information make this usable. We do not assume age, income, neighbourhood, tourism, student pricing or family positioning without evidence.

Option A, recommended for this review: expressive everyday warmth. Advantage: meets the explicit hand-drawn aesthetic request and carries well into illustration and packaging. Tradeoff: raster lettering still needs vector production; operational differentiation remains unproven.

Option B: the geometric clarity of review 01. Advantage: existing editable vector system and straightforward small reproduction. Tradeoff: mechanical expression and little tactile personality. This is a real alternative retained intact, not a rejected owner preference.

Do not build a premium/royal/heritage positioning or promise an authentic regional recipe without evidence. Odisha remains part of the brand name; it does not authorize borrowing sacred or community-specific patterns. One parent brand serves dishes described as information, not new subbrands.

Values become behavior: warmth uses welcoming short phrases; clarity puts dish/portion/allergens before decoration; consistency uses exactly the delivered logo on every application. Reasons to choose the actual food must come from the verified menu, preparation and service. No product superiority claim is supplied.

Validation: confirm the operating menu and service area, then ask five consenting viewers to read the name on a phone and find a dish/portion on a label. Record actual errors and revise. These human tests have not been run. The owner-linked Instagram bio phone now matches Zomato, connecting the researched listings to the brand. Confirm current prices, hours and fulfilment details before making service promises.
