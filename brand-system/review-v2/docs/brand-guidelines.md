# Working brand guidelines — review 02

Start with `../index.html` for a visual walkthrough. This manual documents the proposed system; it does not convert it into an approved identity.

1. For light surfaces choose `assets/logo/primary.png`; for single ink choose `mono.png`; for chilli fields choose `reversed.png`. All are transparent raster exports. For small avatars use the actual lid crop and put the business name in the adjacent account name.
2. Preserve the full name, two lines, E separation, lettering and symbol relationship. Leave at least 8% clearspace; use full name at 180 px or more. Do not stretch, add a badge, alter letter shapes, recreate in a system font or place textured art behind the logo.
3. Chilli and dark ink are the practical text colours. Cream is the main surface. Saffron decorates and may hold ink text; saffron text on cream fails normal-text contrast. Leaf supports illustrations. Tokens are in `../assets/tokens.css`.
4. Georgia headings pair with Arial practical text. Logo art is not a font. Use system fallbacks; do not redistribute font files without a license. English is the tested language scope.
5. Supporting imagery uses gouache/pencil irregularity, one well-composed still life and restrained colour. Caption it as generated illustration where product confusion is possible. It is not proof of menu contents, recipe, preparation or portion.
6. Use the portable HTML applications for editing. Proof PNGs demonstrate the composition. Keep allergens and service facts as data, with no unsupported dietary or registration symbols. Print readiness needs actual vendor inputs.
7. Write warm, plain invitations. Retain “Flavour in Every Layer.” Use @dum_e_odisha, supplied by the owner. The public Instagram bio confirms Bhubaneswar, catering/bulk orders and +91 78942 65632. Keep hours, current menu and origin claims unresolved until verified.
8. Keep current business data in `data/business-profile.json`; keep sourced platform observations in `data/business-research.json`. Before promoting a candidate, save the confirming source and date.

Common errors: shrinking the full logo into an unreadable favicon; presenting the generated packaging scene as a production file; filling placeholders with guessed data; calling a generated drawing human commissioned art; copying a past “final” filename as evidence of approval. Use the decision log and current manifest instead.

Change control: do not overwrite review 01 or original studies. For substantial owner revisions create the next review folder; regenerate affected applications, rerun focused checks and record the exact creative decision. The root home points to the latest review, not an implied approved release.
