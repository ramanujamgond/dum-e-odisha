# Brief — review 02

The problem is a project with hundreds of historical assets but a weak operational center. Review 01 makes the logo editable but its heavy geometric lettering loses the warmth requested by the owner. The new system must improve expression without sacrificing the full name or practical information.

The owner asks for a hand-drawn appearance, not a claim that generated work was drawn by a human. Use tactile brush and pencil cues in supporting images, and strong, legible lettering in the logo. Keep name, existing tagline, warm palette and lifting-lid theme as useful continuity. Remove the boxed E from this new proposal, retain an unmistakable gap, and give Odisha equal visual weight.

Priority contexts: Instagram identity/content (confirmed channel), an order label and takeaway packaging (inferred from existing applications). Customer audience, price tier, kitchen practices, exact menu and delivery market remain unconfirmed. Do not turn a design assumption into a business claim.

Acceptance: one obvious current entry point; primary, single-ink, reverse and compact assets; generated images from both requested tools; source/export separation; working editable templates; public-source research; explicit data gaps; actual browser/render/link checks. Print-ready vectors, supplier dielines, legal review and external publication are outside this review's completion criteria.

Owner supplies final creative decision, operational truth and print/vendor confirmation. Codex supplies a concrete reviewable package and records its technical limits.
