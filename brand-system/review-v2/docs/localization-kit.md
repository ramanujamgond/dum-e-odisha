The exact Unicode strings above are used in live font specimens and preserved in `explorations/directions.json`. A generated image may simplify or distort a virama, matra, conjunct or terminal. Treat those pixels as a lettering sketch; compare with the typeset proof and consult a native reader. A font successfully loading does not constitute linguistic validation.

Keep the E/ए/ଏ separate and readable. Do not turn an icon into a letter or remove script marks to make room for steam. Add the English name as a secondary reading where helpful. Decorative steam, rice and lid forms are secular original design cues, not claims of traditional craft, religious affiliation or regional culinary authenticity.

## Actual font files and rights

Four unmodified Noto font binaries and their matching SIL Open Font License files are bundled under `explorations/fonts/`. They were fetched from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts) on 17 October 2026; exact upstream download URLs, names and hashes are retained in that folder's `provenance.json`. The upstream font family name uses “Oriya”; the interface calls the language Odia. No proprietary system-font binary was copied.

The type lab loads these local font files and allows serif/sans comparison without a network connection. No account, installation or CDN is required. Font licenses remain alongside their files; font availability and outline rendering are distinct from approval of a custom logo.
