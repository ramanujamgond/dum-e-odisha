Verbal identity — review 02

17 October 2026 · Working identity

The name is exactly Dum E Odisha. Category text may say biryani; it does not become “Dum E Biryani Odisha.” Keep “Flavour in Every Layer.” as existing project copy. Instagram is @dum_e_odisha, URL supplied directly by the owner.

Warm: write a simple invitation, “Lift the lid. Make room for flavour.” Avoid excessive exclamation marks and forced food puns. Clear: use an actual dish name, portion and price once verified; avoid “our best-selling signature bestseller” without evidence. Grounded: describe what the owner can deliver; avoid “Odisha's most authentic,” “since 1985,” “royal recipe” and unsupported ingredient claims.

Social draft: “A warm welcome, before the first spoonful.” Destination: https://www.instagram.com/dum_e_odisha/ . This is a brand invitation, not a service guarantee or a customer testimonial.

Operational template: “[Dish name] · [Portion]” with order number, packed date/time and verified allergens. Do not use template examples as an approved menu. Customer recovery example, only if support is later enabled: “Tell us your order number and what went wrong so we can check.” No response-time promise is invented.

When no fact is known, omit the claim or keep the internal field unresolved. Do not turn missing prices into zero, assume the confirmed phone is a WhatsApp number, or insert a guessed recipe story. Odia translation and cultural copy need a qualified speaker; English typography is the tested scope.
