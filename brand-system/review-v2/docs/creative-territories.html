A / The lifted lid — developed recommendation

Large expressive serif wordmark; two clear name lines; lifted-lid/rice symbol; cream fields, chilli red, a small saffron accent. Gouache/pencil still life brings human irregularity to social and packaging while text remains quiet. Emotional target: welcoming anticipation. Full applications are in the guide and applications/.

Strength: recognisable whole name and more character than review 01. Constraint: generated lettering is raster; production reconstruction needs careful optical work. The name must remain readable rather than drifting toward decorative swashes. The small lid is a familiar category cue, so market distinctiveness cannot be claimed without competitive review.

B / Clear geometry — preserved comparison

Actual prior assets: ../../review-v1/assets/logo/primary.svg relative to this document. Review 01 uses bold geometric capitals, separated E tile, abstract rice rhythm and simple information panels. Its source, applications and proof sheet remain available. Strength: font-independent vectors; constraint: a colder, more mechanical tone than the user's current requested aesthetic.

The guide exposes a before/after comparison with both real marks. A is recommended, not approved. No new naming route or borrowed Odisha craft motif is introduced. Existing thirteen studies remain reference history rather than an unmanageable new vote among every past direction.

Later extension / food-brand round 02

The owner supplied new Dribbble and Behance food-logo screenshots and requested more original ideas. Four new routes are developed separately under explorations/round-02/: The Rice Club (character-led), Dum Block (wordmark-led), The Bowl D (negative-space symbol), and The Grain Press (illustration-led). These route labels are creative territory names, not proposed business names. Each logo retains Dum E Odisha.

Each route has a standalone generated logo, a coordinated packaging/brand board, palette, graphic-device rules and tradeoffs on an HTML page. The reference study identifies structural cues observed in the supplied screenshots without asserting their font names, commercial results or permission to reuse the artwork. No new direction has been selected by the owner. Current and script-led explorations remain accessible.
