# Packaging handoff — visual proposal

Priority concepts: paper carrier, squat lidded tub, small seal and card. See `../assets/illustration/packaging-concept.png`. This generated mockup is a visual study; it is not editable packaging artwork or evidence of actual manufacturing.

Hierarchy: full canonical logo on a clean cream face, supporting illustration on the lower third, saffron on a small seal, operational information on a separate quiet label. Keep the full name clear around handles, folds and the tub seam. Do not treat generated mockup lettering as the source for the logo.

Needed from vendor: final physical sizes, approved food-contact substrate, print method and colour profile, actual flat dielines, bleed and safe zones, fold/glue areas, lid/body mapping and production tolerances. Needed from owner: verified menu/order content, food-label requirements and any applicable registrations. Unknown inputs are not filled with decorative fictitious data.

Production sequence: approve identity → reconstruct vector mark → receive dielines → place exact locked artwork → proof information and seam placement → obtain print sample → inspect ink, small text, folds and grease/handling behavior → approve manufacture. No part of this review establishes that these tests have happened.

The 900 × 550 HTML order label is a useful layout demonstration. It has no millimetre-size, adhesive, bleed or compliance guarantee. A regulated product label requires specialist review of actual operational facts and applicable requirements.
