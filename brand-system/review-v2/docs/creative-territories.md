# Creative territories — review 02

## A / The lifted lid — developed recommendation

Large expressive serif wordmark; two clear name lines; lifted-lid/rice symbol; cream fields, chilli red, a small saffron accent. Gouache/pencil still life brings human irregularity to social and packaging while text remains quiet. Emotional target: welcoming anticipation. Full applications are in the guide and `applications/`.

Strength: recognisable whole name and more character than review 01. Constraint: generated lettering is raster; production reconstruction needs careful optical work. The name must remain readable rather than drifting toward decorative swashes. The small lid is a familiar category cue, so market distinctiveness cannot be claimed without competitive review.
