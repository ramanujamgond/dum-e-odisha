Useful design evidence

Bombay 404's own Jaani Maani Biryani case study describes narrative packaging showing people sharing food, with Indian-comics influences and street-stall lettering. Design inference: a memorable biryani identity can put everyday shared meals into supporting illustration while keeping the name readable. Use that principle, not its characters, layout or wordmark.

Pentagram's Super Peach case study documents hand-drawn marks and illustration by Pol Montserrat, paired with functional supporting typography and a consistent palette across menus, bags and uniforms. Design inference: expressive drawing works best with a disciplined information hierarchy. The generated artwork in this project should be described as generated illustration with a hand-drawn appearance; it is not evidence of an illustrator's authorship.

SIDAC's Crafts of Odisha identifies Saura painting with specific communities and ritual meaning. The Government of Odisha account of Jhoti, Chita and Muruja distinguishes rice-paste wall/floor drawing from coloured powder work and describes their ceremonial context. Design inference: original rice-ear, leaf, steam and table scenes are suitable neutral motifs. Do not label generic geometric art as authentic Saura, reproduce a sacred diagram as a restaurant mascot, or claim a community partnership without evidence.

Completion rules for missing business data

Populate the Instagram URL, first-party phone, city and catering/bulk-enquiry claims from I1. The matched platform links are useful external destinations; do not promise a current delivery slot or successful checkout. Keep the existing project line separate from the observed Instagram bio line so neither is falsely described as owner-approved new positioning. Detailed menu, prices, address, hours, allergens, dietary labels and regulatory claims retain their field-specific limits. Confirm the current certificate or regulator record before printing a license claim. Research-backed design recommendations can be completed without inventing founder history, recipes, sourcing or operational facts.
