Exact prompts and conditioning paths: `../sources/generation-prompts.json`. This is evidence of actual executed generations, not merely a prompt pack. Prior review-v1 statements that Nano Banana was unavailable are historical; the dedicated MCP worked in this session.

Medium: gouache and red coloured pencil on ivory paper. Visible dry brushwork, imperfect contours, sparse hatching, terracotta and individual rice grains. Support colours: chilli, saffron, leaf, cream. No photoreal food substitute, glossy 3D, arbitrary spice collage, fake metallic effects or unexplained sacred/traditional motifs.

Inspection: the food still life has a distinct pot/lid/spoon, legible rice texture and no stray text. It also contains chicken-like forms; it is not suitable as verified vegetarian/dietary imagery and does not establish any actual recipe or serving size. The logo has readable words with a deliberate DUM/E gap. Packaging repeats the proposed identity but generated perspective/text is a visual approximation; use canonical assets in actual artwork.

Rights ledger: new art is generated by requested tools; no third-party logo or stock photo is imported into the artwork. No claim of exclusive rights, registered identity or trademark clearance. Applicable service terms and the owner's intended commercial use should be checked before release. Reference case-study images were not downloaded or republished. For the later script explorations, four Noto font files are bundled with their OFL licenses, and the two owner-supplied screenshots are retained as local reference material; see localization-kit.html. Uncoated paper in the mockup is a visual material choice, not a verified sustainable or food-safe claim.

New image briefs should retain the palette/medium and one clear still life. Keep name and operational text separate from generated art. Real menu photography must show the actual served portion with permission and should be labeled separately from these illustrations.
