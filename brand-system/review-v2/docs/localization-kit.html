Prepared 17 October 2026. The owner requested additional logo ideas based on two supplied reference screenshots, including Odia and Hindi lettering, and more wall/banner/TV applications. The original English business name remains Dum E Odisha. These are alternatives for review; no winner or final script spelling has been approved.

View and compare

Idea collection · Odia & Hindi type lab · Reference analysis.

Four direction pages are available: the existing lifted-lid proposal with new applications; a new Latin letter-and-lid idea; an Odia-led name; and a Hindi-led sign-painted name. Each page includes a generated wall concept and exact digital compositions for a 1920 × 640 banner, 1920 × 1080 TV holding slide and 900 × 1800 tall poster. PNG exports and editable HTML layouts are linked from each page. None of these pixel canvases establishes a physical print size, dieline or screen overscan specification.

The exact Unicode strings above are used in live font specimens and preserved in explorations/directions.json. A generated image may simplify or distort a virama, matra, conjunct or terminal. Treat those pixels as a lettering sketch; compare with the typeset proof and consult a native reader. A font successfully loading does not constitute linguistic validation.

Keep the E/ए/ଏ separate and readable. Do not turn an icon into a letter or remove script marks to make room for steam. Add the English name as a secondary reading where helpful. Decorative steam, rice and lid forms are secular original design cues, not claims of traditional craft, religious affiliation or regional culinary authenticity.

Actual font files and rights

Four unmodified Noto font binaries and their matching SIL Open Font License files are bundled under explorations/fonts/. They were fetched from the official Google Fonts repository on 17 October 2026; exact upstream download URLs, names and hashes are retained in that folder's provenance.json. The upstream font family name uses “Oriya”; the interface calls the language Odia. No proprietary system-font binary was copied.

The type lab loads these local font files and allows serif/sans comparison without a network connection. No account, installation or CDN is required. Font licenses remain alongside their files; font availability and outline rendering are distinct from approval of a custom logo.

Required native review

Reviewer: not yet assigned; no native-speaker approval is claimed. The owner should nominate an Odia reader and a Hindi reader familiar with local naming conventions. Verify intended pronunciation, preferred spelling, virama/matra placement, script joins, cultural associations and small-sign legibility. Record their exact preferred string and review date before vectorizing the selected mark. No back-translation or machine-generated artwork is treated as proof of suitability.

Provenance and checks

New logo sketches and alternative wall scenes: Nano Banana MCP, Gemini 3 Pro Image. Current-identity mural: built-in image_gen conditioned on the existing logo and food illustration. Prompt records are in explorations/generation-prompts.json and wall-generation-prompts.json. User screenshots are reference material attributed in-image to graphic_dada, not reusable brand assets.

Digital display PNGs are browser-rendered compositions that reuse the exact supplied route image rather than asking generation to retype it. The underlying logo remains raster. The screenshot-based wall concepts may approximate lettering and are not installed premises. Actual browser checks and exports are recorded in explorations/proofs/browser-checks.json.

Next decision: select an idea or request a focused refinement after viewing each route in context. Adoption, native review, vector reconstruction and physical production remain separate from these completed exploratory files.
