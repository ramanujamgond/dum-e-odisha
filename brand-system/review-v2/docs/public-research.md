## Useful design evidence

Bombay 404's own [Jaani Maani Biryani case study](https://www.bombay404.com/work/jaanimaanibiryani) describes narrative packaging showing people sharing food, with Indian-comics influences and street-stall lettering. **Design inference:** a memorable biryani identity can put everyday shared meals into supporting illustration while keeping the name readable. Use that principle, not its characters, layout or wordmark.

Pentagram's [Super Peach case study](https://www.pentagram.com/work/super-peach) documents hand-drawn marks and illustration by Pol Montserrat, paired with functional supporting typography and a consistent palette across menus, bags and uniforms. **Design inference:** expressive drawing works best with a disciplined information hierarchy. The generated artwork in this project should be described as generated illustration with a hand-drawn appearance; it is not evidence of an illustrator's authorship.

SIDAC's [Crafts of Odisha](https://sidac.odisha.gov.in/en/crafts-of-odisha) identifies Saura painting with specific communities and ritual meaning. The [Government of Odisha account of Jhoti, Chita and Muruja](https://www.energy.odisha.gov.in/en/odisha-tourism/jhoti-chita-muruja) distinguishes rice-paste wall/floor drawing from coloured powder work and describes their ceremonial context. **Design inference:** original rice-ear, leaf, steam and table scenes are suitable neutral motifs. Do not label generic geometric art as authentic Saura, reproduce a sacred diagram as a restaurant mascot, or claim a community partnership without evidence.
