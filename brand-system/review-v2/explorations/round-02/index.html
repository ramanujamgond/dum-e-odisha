New / Food-brand explorations / Round 02

Four ideas.
Four different personalities.

A mascot, a bold wordmark, a compact symbol and a tactile illustrated identity. Each direction is developed for Dum E Odisha with its own packaging, graphic language and colour system.

Also available: the focused Dum Club refinement, preserving the owner’s original rounded name and lid while exploring negative space and measured golden-ratio layout.

01 / CHARACTER-LED

The Rice Club

A rice-grain host turns the brand into a friendly character. Blue and butter yellow build a recognisable world of stickers, packaging and hand-drawn moments.

View logo & brand world ↗
02 / WORDMARK-LED

Dum Block

The full name becomes a solid typographic shape. Broad custom lettering and a measured rhythm of red and saffron stripes carry the identity across bags, tubs and signs.

View logo & brand world ↗
03 / SYMBOL-LED

The Bowl D

A sturdy D holds a bowl-shaped counter. The compact symbol can lead seals and avatars, while a complete wordmark keeps the business name clear.

View logo & brand world ↗
04 / ILLUSTRATION-LED

The Grain Press

An original rice-ear illustration pairs with strong editorial serif lettering. The brand feels like a small food journal printed on uncoated paper, with generous margins and botanical details.
