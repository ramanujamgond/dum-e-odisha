Round 02 / The supplied screenshots

What the references
actually suggest.

These screenshots contain several different kinds of food identity. The new concepts explore those structural differences through original artwork for Dum E Odisha.

Character as the identity

The Slurpee and Food for Thought examples in the Dribbble screenshot use expressive illustrated characters. Tommy’s combines drawn personality and lettering. The Behance screenshot shows friendly character-led systems in Ornico, Bunny Burger and other examples. The transferable idea is a recognisable character with a repeatable personality, not a copied pose or species.

New route: The Rice Club—an original rice-grain host, blue ink, butter-yellow accents and a family of character moments.

The name as a solid shape

CHESINI, Mello, Sika and Chiso make letter weight, counters and the word’s silhouette carry recognition. Different lettering styles lead to different brand moods. The new proposal studies compact weight and packaging impact while retaining a readable gap before E.

New route: Dum Block—an original two-line uppercase wordmark and a measured stripe system.

A compact idea in negative space

The Dribbble screenshot includes C-and-bowl and geometric utensil studies. Their useful principle is economy: one memorable silhouette, one clear internal idea. A small mark should still work when the surrounding presentation is removed.

New route: The Bowl D—a different D silhouette with a bowl-shaped counter, paired with the full business name.

A quieter illustrated brand world

MORK’s illustrated packaging and Lume’s controlled colour blocks show how material, illustration and typography can build a system together. The principle is consistency across touchpoints rather than adding decoration to one logo.

New route: The Grain Press—an original rice-ear drawing, editorial lettering and an uncoated-paper direction.

Source and limits

The screenshots were supplied by the owner from Dribbble and Behance search results. Visible project names identify the reference tiles; this study does not verify their authorship, effectiveness, font names or commercial rights. No source logo or packaging artwork is reused. Advertisements, site controls and account details are irrelevant to the design brief.
