What stays fixed.

Keep every character and mark in the proposed name intact. Symbol accents must sit outside the letters, matras and script joins. A small English name underneath can support readers unfamiliar with the script.

Odia proposal: ଦମ୍ ଏ ଓଡ଼ିଶା. Hindi proposal: दम ए ओडिशा. These are draft transliterations, not native-reviewed final names. Verify the preferred spelling, pronunciation and matra/virama rendering with an Odia/Hindi reader before adopting a logo.

Font sources & licensing
