# Verbal identity — review v1
Name: **Dum E Odisha** in prose; **DUM E ODISHA** in artwork. Category: biryani. Existing tagline: **Flavour in Every Layer.** Do not merge category into a new business name.

Voice: warm through direct invitations (“Open the lid. Enjoy the moment.”); clear through concrete nouns (“Choose your biryani”); considerate through factual service messages. Avoid “royal authentic perfection,” fake urgency, exaggerated heritage or “Odisha’s best.” Warmth never overrides safety or accurate order information.

Message order: brand → category → verified dish/portion/price → verified next action. An attractive illustration is supporting imagery, not evidence of ingredients or serving size.

## Ready-to-adapt copy
Website hero: “Flavour in Every Layer.” Supporting line: “Dum E Odisha · Biryani.” CTA once a real menu exists: “Explore the menu.” Do not publish an inactive ordering button.
Social: “Open the lid. Enjoy the moment.” Caption: “Dum E Odisha. Flavour in Every Layer.” Add only a verified ordering destination, never a fabricated offer.
Order label: “[Dish name] / [Portion] / [Order number] / Packed: [date and time] / Allergens: [verified information].” These are intentional operator fields.
Unavailable item: “This item is unavailable right now. Please choose another option.” Do not promise a return time without an operational source.
Delay: “Your order is taking longer than expected. We’ll share an updated time when it’s confirmed.” Use only when the business can actually provide updates.
Complaint: “I’m sorry your order wasn’t right. Please share your order number so we can check what happened.” Never promise an unapproved refund or ask for unnecessary personal information.

Readability: sentence case, short sentences, one action at a time. Show dietary categories in words, not only colored dots. Keep preparation, hygiene, halal/organic, provenance, service time and allergen claims out until verified. Do not translate into Odia by approximation; a fluent reviewer must approve spelling, nuance and font coverage.
