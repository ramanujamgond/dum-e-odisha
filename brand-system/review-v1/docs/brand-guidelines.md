# Working brand guidelines — review v1 / not approved for release
## Choose the right file
For cream/light fields: primary SVG. For simple dark ink on cream: mono SVG. For chilli fields: reversed SVG. At tiny sizes: compact SVG plus adjacent full name. For a social post: edit `applications/social-portrait.svg`; for an order label: edit `applications/order-label.svg`. Never distribute a proof sheet as the logo master.

## Color roles
Chilli `#852D26`: identity, headings, active elements. Cream `#FFF6E7`: primary surface. Saffron `#E8A535`: decorative accent, E tile, rice rhythm. Ink `#29231F`: operational text. Values are sRGB digital specifications. Saffron is not body text on cream. Use words plus symbols for dietary or status information. See measured pairings in validation report; palette checks alone are not whole-product accessibility certification.

## Type and layout
Logo: custom path geometry, no font installation needed. Application text: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif fallback; preview rendered with locally available Arial. No font binary redistributed. Use system fonts on the web; obtain license evidence before embedding a font in another deliverable. Odia coverage is not asserted.

Applications use an 8 px spacing rhythm, 20 px corner radius where useful, generous margins and a maximum of two text weights. Browser body copy 18 px, 1.5 line height; secondary information no smaller than 14 px. Label master is 900 × 550; keep content inside its fields. The digital template is not a dieline.

## Image and copy rules
Use the illustration as brand atmosphere, with a caption identifying it as illustration where product confusion is possible. Actual menu photography should show the served item and needs rights and portion verification. Keep text separate from raster art. Crop around the pot; do not remove the steam or put small type over textured food. Preserve the supplied brand name and existing tagline. Avoid fabricated benefits or origin stories.

## Practical misuse
Wrong: saffron body text on cream. Right: chilli/ink text, saffron ornament.
Wrong: logo squeezed into a narrow label. Right: full logo at sufficient width or compact mark + text.
Wrong: claim a JPEG mockup is packaging production artwork. Right: provide SVG art, vendor dieline, substrate proof and approved content separately.
Wrong: treat generated rice as an exact dish photo. Right: label it editorial art and use verified food photography for menu choices.

## Governance
Owner approves exact v1 hashes in manifest. Change name, palette or lettering only in a new version; regenerate applications, inspect all variants and update rights/test records. Keep original material in `brand/`. This draft manual does not grant publication authorization.
