# Creative directions — review v1
Existing 13 routes are preserved in the legacy gallery. Reopening all 13 would increase ambiguity rather than solve the production gap.

A / **Clear lid** — developed review candidate. Chunky custom geometric lettering; physically separated E tile; tilted lid; chilli dominant and saffron as a small accent. Cream whitespace keeps food information calm. On labels, logo occupies the left and editable order content the right. On social, the full name sits above a generous headline and rice rhythm. Benefit: small-format coherence; tradeoff: less expressive than the previous serif raster.

B / **Quiet type** — comparison study in `sources/alternative-type-only.svg`. No pot or E tile; a compact one-ink typographic name. Label use would allocate more room to food data; social use would rely on photography for food cues. Benefit: simple production, reduced imagery dependence; tradeoff: loses the existing lid and E equity. This is an exploratory vector study, not a developed second brand kit.

Recommend A for owner review, because it preserves more observed equity and fixes editability. It is not a pixel-identical vector tracing. Owner may retain the existing serif JPEG direction and commission a closer typographic reconstruction instead. No winner has been approved.

Imagery: the new editorial handi is deliberately separate from the logo, giving applications texture without degrading name recognition. Abstract rice rhythm is original geometry and does not claim Saura, Pattachitra, Pipili or Jhoti authorship.
