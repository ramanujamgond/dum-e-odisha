The Dum Keeper

Modern illustrative mascot · care & aroma

Best for immediate food-service recognition and a compact, friendly symbol.

The story in the symbol.

A cook holds a handi close, with the face and aroma shaped through negative space. The gesture communicates care and anticipation of the meal.

Brand name: Dum E Odisha.
Tagline: Flavour in Every Layer.

Positioning: A biryani specialist bringing flavour and variety to everyday cravings and shared meals.

Personality: Personable, confident, welcoming.
Concept promise: Good food, warmly served.

The illustration becomes a brand world

From symbol to packaging.

Use the host as a recognisable recurring character on labels and social posts. Supporting illustrations can show serving and sharing; keep the face, pot and main pose consistent.

Negative space and main background

HEX colours are design targets. Generated raster tones may vary. Use the main colours consistently rather than changing the logo for every biryani flavour.

Words & typography.

Rounded sans lettering with a clear functional sans-serif for menu copy. The generated lettering is artwork; no exact licensed font match is claimed.

Write the full name Dum E Odisha. Keep Dum and E visibly separate. “Biryani” is a category or product description, outside the brand name.

Voice: simple, welcoming and food-led. Example: “Find your favourite. Share a biryani.” Keep Flavour in Every Layer. as the shared tagline across these concept routes.

Working use rules

  • Lead with the illustration; keep the name readable beneath it.
  • Preserve the source composition, colours and proportions.
  • Keep at least a symbol-head or blossom width of clear space around the full logo.
  • Use plain fields behind the logo; place larger illustration on separate panels.
  • Do not place intricate storytelling inside the small symbol.
  • Actual-size legibility, small-format simplification and one-ink versions require a further artwork pass after a route is chosen.

Reference, interpreted.

An original illustrated host. The supplied chef references informed negative-space simplicity; their specific silhouettes, moustaches and hats are not the identity.
