Three reference additions.

Aroma Paisley, the original hands-and-handi direction (now called Generous Handi), and Dum Club have been added as concepts 11–13. Their source artworks are preserved, with the public name corrected to Dum E Odisha and clear Dum/E spacing.

Each addition has a packaging scene, a restaurant feature wall and a banner mockup. These are concept settings, not an existing venue or fabrication specification.

What stays consistent

The name is Dum E Odisha, with a clear separation around E. The tagline is Flavour in Every Layer. Each route has its own palette and illustration grammar; the routes are alternatives rather than interchangeable brand variants.

Geometry and delivery

The Spiral of Dum is a golden-ratio-inspired geometric study. The generated raster is not evidence of exact φ proportions. A documented vector construction would be required for that claim.

All new visuals were generated through Nano Banana MCP. Logos and mockups are visually checked for spelling, separation, meaning and consistency. Local files and guide references are validated; no fresh browser-render check is claimed. Raster concepts need production masters and physical proofs before manufacture.
