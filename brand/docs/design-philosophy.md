# Shared Warmth

Rounded, substantial forms should feel as welcoming as a meal set down between friends. Preserve the generous curves and deliberate asymmetry of the selected cooking-lid wordmark. The craft lies in smooth junctions, open counters and balanced visual weight; no extra ornament should compete with the name.

Colour should describe structure. A saffron lid rests above chilli lettering, while open white space makes both forms easy to distinguish. Two inks carry the identity. Dark neutral text belongs to supporting information, and a cream field can warm an application without becoming another logo colour.

Hierarchy should communicate the full name before any promise. Dum E and Odisha belong together as the brand; the tagline is a quieter optional expression. Every line needs deliberate spacing. Careful alignment and consistent proportions matter more than imposing a decorative mathematical grid on generated artwork.

Applications should prove that the same identity survives use. A lid label, delivery sleeve or social post should repeat the chosen artwork and its colour roles. Restrained layout, precise margins and honest product imagery create a coherent experience. The result should show painstaking attention in its spacing and typography rather than added effects.
