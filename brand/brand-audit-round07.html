Meaning in the illustration.

The supplied references combine a recognisable subject with a second idea through negative space. The new concepts use that principle to tell stories about a meal.

Kruti Coffee

Kruti’s official story describes its work with coffee farmers in Kindiriguda, Odisha. Its useful lesson here is the connection between the brand and a grounded story. Dum E Odisha’s brief supports flavour, variety and shared meals; no farmer partnership or social-impact programme has been assumed.

Odisha folk inspiration

Saura is a specific Odisha tradition with ritual context. Warli is a different tradition associated with Maharashtra. The supplied red-and-white image is used as a broad visual reference, not copied or presented as evidence of Odisha provenance.

The Shared Handi uses original people, rice and cooking forms to tell a contemporary meal story. It is not presented as an authentic ritual painting or artisan collaboration.

Mughal botanical inspiration

The Met documents floral compositions from the Mughal court, including a composite plant made from different botanical parts. The Dum Garden translates balanced stems and blossoms into a modern handi illustration. It makes no historical recipe claim.
