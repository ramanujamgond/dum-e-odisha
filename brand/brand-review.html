Dum E Odisha / 13 distinct identities / Revision 10

Thirteen stories.
One love of biryani.

Traditional craft references and contemporary symbols, explored through thirteen different logos and complete visual directions.

Each concept has its own logo, colour palette, type direction, illustration language, use rules and packaging mockup. All thirteen identities now include packaging, wall-design and banner mockups. The first ten also include a second sharing-meal packaging scene. The full name remains Dum E Odisha; the common tagline is Flavour in Every Layer.
