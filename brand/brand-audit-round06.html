Clarity comes first.

The previous logo blurred “Dum” and “E”. More colour could not repair that structural problem.

What changed

Earlier designFinal design
Joined, flowing lowercase “dume”DUM capitals and independent E block with real negative space

Primary-source research

Visually reviewed the colour logo, reverse proof and both packaging images for name spelling, DUM/E separation, Odisha hierarchy and consistent colour placement. A black study retained a tinted tagline and was excluded from the final download package.

Files and local guide references are validated separately. Browser preview was unavailable, so no fresh browser-render check is claimed. The raster set establishes the final visual design; vector masters, exact ink separations and physical print proofs remain a production stage.
